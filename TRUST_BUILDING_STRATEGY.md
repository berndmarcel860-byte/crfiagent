# Trust-Building Strategy for BaFin-Licensed Fund Recovery Platform

## Executive Summary

**Challenge:** Many scam companies copy legitimate business structures, making it difficult for clients to distinguish between genuine BaFin-licensed services and fraudulent operations.

**Solution:** Comprehensive trust-building documentation package sent with welcome email.
